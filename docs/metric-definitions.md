# Metric Definitions

- Trips processed: count of records accepted into Silver after deterministic validation.
- Quarantined records: count of records rejected by a documented quality rule.
- Average duration: arithmetic mean of positive trip durations in minutes.
- Route performance: origin-destination aggregate over validated Silver trips.
- Revenue metrics: source-supported fare, tip, toll, and total amount aggregates. Unsupported source fields must be omitted, not fabricated.
